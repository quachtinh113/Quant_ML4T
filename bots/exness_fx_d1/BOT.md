## Hypothesis

**Approved by user 2026-09-06** (the mentor's draft of 2026-09-06, adopted as final text; the builder's earlier
proposal and the "Draft for the user" blocks were removed on the same day, Decisions log):

*The five dollar pairs share one factor (2.4 independent bets, `01` §B.2), so the bot does not look for a
cross-sectional ranking edge first. Its edge, if any, is (i) carry: the policy-rate differential is compensation for
holding a pair, earned slowly and lost quickly in dollar shocks (Ch08 §8.3; `fx_pairs/03` known limitations); and
(ii) the slow re-pricing of non-dollar legs after a US-session move, read per pair against its own history
(time-series framing, Ch16 08). The counterparty is flow that must trade regardless of price (hedgers, fix-driven
rebalancing). It is falsified when, at the cumulative trial count in this table, the validation DSR of the best
long-short or per-pair book on active returns over the 1/N long book is below the declared level, or when the
breakeven cost from `16_costs` is below the p90 spread measured on the real Pro account. The `fx_pairs` prior (no
resolved spot-only edge on 20 pairs) stands until then.*

Declared level (user decision 2026-09-06): the notebook convention of `16_strategy_simulation/12_dsr_validation.py`,
`confidence_level = 0.95`, i.e. `is_significant = probability >= 0.95`; statistics are reported on the ACTIVE return
(strategy minus the 1/N long book of the five pairs) as well as against SR = 0. Context the hypothesis has to beat:
the template found no resolved cross-sectional edge on 20 pairs with price-and-volume features alone
(`case_studies/fx_pairs/README.md`, Key Results), and this bot's own baseline (`02_labels` §G, trailing 126-session
return against `fwd_ret_1d`) has mean IC −0.0016, HAC t −0.14, p 0.892 over 2,069 sessions.

## Kill criteria

**Approved by user 2026-09-06** (the mentor's draft of 2026-09-06, adopted as final text; written before the first
live order):

Pause when (a) drawdown from peak > 8 % of allocated capital, or (b) the rolling 63-session hit rate of the deployed
book is below 0.5 for 21 consecutive sessions (an IC over five names is not a readable statistic), or (c) the
realised round-trip cost on the demo/live tape exceeds 1.5× the assumed 2.6 bp for a calendar month, or (d)
`SafeBroker` reconciliation finds an unexplained position, or (e) the decision bar is missing within tolerance (no
order that day; the 2018-01-31 outage rule), or (f) the realised swap per night exceeds the value declared in
`setup.yaml::costs.swap` for a week. Retire when the latest re-estimate of the breakeven cost (`16_costs`) is below
the measured p90 spread or the holdout PSR against the 1/N book is below 0.5 (the benchmark of a book of dollar pairs
is the 1/N long book, not zero). Account-level breakers (equity, margin level, open orders) live in
`bots/_shared/monitor/` and stop every bot at once, before the Exness stop-out.
