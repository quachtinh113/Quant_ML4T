## Hypothesis

> **DRAFT FOR THE USER - NOT APPROVED.** Written 2026-09-08 by the builder from the phase-1 evidence below. It is a
> proposal, not a decision, and no phase-5 backtest may be run against it until the user approves or replaces it.

*The return of a US index splits into two processes that a single daily label averages together (Ch08 s8.1;
`bots/assets/US500.md` s10). The **overnight** leg carries the beta: it collects the earnings releases, the foreign
session and the futures-led repricing, and on the development sample it earns +5.8 bps (US500) and +7.5 bps (USTEC)
per session against +2.6 and +3.5 for the intraday leg. The **intraday** leg is where the opening auction is worked
off and the closing auction is met, and it mean-reverts: measured 2026-09-08 on 1,676 development index-sessions the
within-index lag-1 autocorrelation of `fwd_ret_intraday` is **-0.1146** (pooled se 0.0128, about nine standard
errors), and shorting the previous session's intraday move earns an information coefficient of +0.127 (US500) and
+0.102 (USTEC) with no model at all. The counterparty is flow that must trade on a clock rather than on a price:
end-of-day rebalancing, MOC imbalance (`US500.md` s3) and the opening auction's own overshoot. The bot forecasts the
two legs with separate per-index time-series models and takes a position only where its own forecast clears a
declared threshold. It is refuted when, at the cumulative trial count K, the Deflated Sharpe Ratio of the selected
spec on the **active return over a 1/N long book measured on the same session window** is below 0.95, or when the
breakeven cost falls below the p90 spread measured on the real account.*

Prior this has to beat: `nasdaq100_microstructure`, where a raw intraday signal on this same basket lost to costs
(`bots/assets/USTEC.md` s10).

> **CORRECTION, 2026-09-08 (second pass).** The sentence that stood here - *the round-trip spread is small (1.32 /
> 0.78 bps), so this bot's cost problem is financing on the overnight leg, not the spread* - **is false over half
> the development sample** and is withdrawn. It was written from a thirty-day tick window measured in
> August-September 2026. Read on this account's own H1 tape at the execution bars, the p90 spread is 3.2-3.9 bps
> from 2022 to 2024-10 and 0.85-1.05 bps after it. The declared round trip is therefore **8.10 bps**, not 1.32 /
> 0.78, against a median session move of 29-49 bps: 84-91 % of development sessions still move further than the
> round trip, so the bot is not dead on cost, but the spread is a first-order cost over the older half of the
> sample and the falsification test has to be read against 8.10 bps rather than 2.1. The financing claim about the
> overnight leg stands unchanged: -1.91 / -2.01 bps a night long, three times that on a Friday, nothing short.

## Kill criteria

> **DRAFT FOR THE USER - NOT APPROVED.** Written 2026-09-08, mirroring the approved set of `exness_fx_d1`
> (`bots/exness_fx_d1/BOT.md`). No live order may be placed before the user approves or replaces these.

Pause, automatically and without a human, when any of:

(a) drawdown from peak > **8 %** of allocated capital;
(b) rolling 63-session hit rate < 0.5 for 21 consecutive sessions, **counted separately per spec** (intraday and
    overnight are different processes and one must not mask the other);
(c) realised round-trip cost > **1.5x** the assumed cost over one month;
(d) reconciliation between the run record and `positions_get` disagrees;
(e) a decision instant has no bar to fill at -> no order that session (a normal state for the overnight spec, not an
    incident; it is a kill criterion only if it starts happening to the intraday spec);
(f) realised swap per night > `setup.yaml::costs.swap` over one week;
(g) the daily break stops ending at 18:00 New York, i.e. the trading hours stop following
    `decision.trade_hours_follow_dst_of` (checked on the DST changeover weeks).

Retire when `16_costs` re-estimates the breakeven below the p90 spread measured on the **real** account, or when the
holdout PSR against a 1/N long book on the same session window is below 0.5.
